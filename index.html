The Problem

Coaching and training run on disconnected tools

Most fitness apps solve one piece. None of them close the loop between what an athlete does and what a coach sees in the moment.

1

No real-time loop

A trainer writes a plan, then finds out weeks later — or never — whether it was actually followed correctly.

2

Form errors go uncorrected

Most injuries in strength and racket/ball sports trace back to posture errors nobody caught in the moment.

3

Athletes fall back on Excel

Current tracking apps don't feel like enough, so serious athletes end up in spreadsheets anyway.

4

Coaches drown in data

Coaches running dozens of clients are overwhelmed managing progress across scattered spreadsheets.

The Solution

One platform where every rep an athlete logs, a coach sees instantly

P2P Network

Short alphanumeric IDs connect athletes and coaches directly — search, verify, and link in seconds. Never required to use the tracker.

Live Tracking

Every set, run, or lap syncs to the coach's screen the moment it happens. No lag, no weekly check-in.

AI Form Coach

Computer vision reads your movement mid-set and flags posture issues before they become injuries.

Best-in-class as a standalone tracker from day one — the P2P coach layer is optional, never required.

What's Built In

Beyond the sync — a complete training platform

✓

Verified PRs & Progress

Lift history, PRs, and body composition tracking that updates itself from every logged workout — set once, tracked forever.

✓

Multi-Language Coaching Videos

Gym Mapping for Travelers

Wearable & Nutrition Integration

✓

Privacy by Default

Automatic face-blurring on shared clips, and granular control over exactly who sees your data.
