The Problem

A $100B+ industry runs on spreadsheets, DMs, and outdated tools

Every one of these unsolved problems is a massive market opportunity waiting for a platform to fix it.

1

No real-time loop

A trainer writes a plan, then finds out weeks later — or never — whether it was actually followed correctly.

→ Opportunity: Real-time sync creates accountability and better results.

2

Form errors go uncorrected

Most injuries in strength and racket/ball sports trace back to posture and contact-angle errors nobody caught in the moment.

→ Opportunity: AI-powered form correction reduces injuries and improves outcomes.

3

Trust is one-sided

Pay for 10 sessions, don't like session 2 — most platforms and gyms still keep 100% of your money.

→ Opportunity: Escrow payments and refundable wallets build user trust.

4

Discovery is broken

Finding a verified, right-fit coach is word-of-mouth or a generic directory with no proof of results.

→ Opportunity: Verified coach profiles and transparent results drive discovery.

5

Athletes fall back on Excel

Current tracking apps don't feel like enough, so serious athletes end up managing their own spreadsheets anyway.

→ Opportunity: A complete, all-in-one platform that eliminates spreadsheets forever.

6

Coaches drown in data

Coaches running dozens of clients are overwhelmed managing progress across scattered spreadsheets with no real system underneath it.

→ Opportunity: A unified dashboard that scales coaching from 1 to 100 clients.

This list grows as we talk to more athletes and coaches — it's not a fixed set.
