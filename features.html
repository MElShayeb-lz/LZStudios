The Platform

Beyond the sync — a complete training ecosystem

The real-time loop is the core, not the whole product. Here's everything DYEL does — and will do.

TIER 1: THE NETWORK (PHASE 1)

✓

Real-Time Sync

Every set logs to the coach's screen the moment it happens. No lag, no weekly check-ins.

✓

Verified PRs & Progress

Lift history, PRs, and body composition tracking that updates itself from every logged workout.

✓

P2P Coach Matching

Verified coaches with transparent credentials and results. Athletes find the right fit — fast.

✓

Privacy by Default

Automatic face-blurring on shared clips, and granular control over exactly who sees your data.

TIER 2: ORGANIZATIONAL TOOLS (PHASE 2)

→

Escrow Payments & Wallets

Pay for coaching with confidence — unused sessions refund to a wallet instead of disappearing.

→

Org Layers (Gyms, Schools, Teams)

Gyms, schools, and coaching orgs can manage all their athletes and coaches from one dashboard.

→

Gym Mapping for Travelers

Scan a gym once — equipment and calibrated load data are saved so future visitors never have to rescan.

→

Wearable & Nutrition Integration

Connects with the fitness trackers and nutrition logging tools you already use, in one unified profile.

TIER 3: THE AI ENGINE (PHASE 3)

✦

AI Form Correction

Computer vision runs during every rep — not after. Flags posture issues before they become injuries.

✦

Multi-Language Coaching Videos

Verified workout demos in 7 core languages, matched to a coach with a similar build to you for relevance.

✦

Pose Generation

AI generates video of YOU doing the movement correctly — using your actual body and face.

✦

Contact Angle Analysis

For ball and racket sports — AI analyzes optimal angles for strikes, throws, and punches.

The moat: Each tier builds on the last. Network density → organization lock-in → AI defensibility.
